Key: stack written bottom to top. Tree1 = green. Tree2 = brown+green. Tree3 = brown+brown+green.
Mtn1/2/3 = 1/2/3 gray tokens. Bld = red on top of any brown/gray/red (always 2 high). Y = yellow. W = blue.
`*` = the token the animal cube goes on.
Ladder = points per cube space, top to bottom. Score = topmost space with no cube; all cubes on = 0.
Shape: PAIR = 2 adjacent. LINE = 3 in a straight line. BEND = 3 with the middle one off the line (V shape). TRI = 2 stacked side by side plus 1 touching both. DIAMOND = 4 (top, bottom, left, right).
